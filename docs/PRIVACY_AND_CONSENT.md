# Privacy and consent

This is guidance for users and contributors, not legal advice.

## Our promises

- Audio and transcripts stay on your device by default.
- No account, no API key and no telemetry are required. Anything that sends data out is optional, off by default, and clearly marked.
- Storage is local, with one-click delete of any recording or transcript.

## Your responsibility when recording

Laws on recording differ. Some places require everyone's consent (for example several US states), and rules such as GDPR can apply to stored voice data. Some workplaces and meeting platforms also have their own rules.

- Tell participants before you record, and get their agreement where the law or the platform requires it.
- Do not use the software to record people secretly.
- Do not share transcripts of other people without their permission.

The app will show a reminder before every recording and a visible recording indicator.

## For contributors and testers

Never commit real call recordings. Test audio must be synthetic or recorded with written consent from everyone in it, and must carry a license that allows redistribution.
