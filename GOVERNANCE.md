# Governance

- The founding maintainer (@myviapps) makes final decisions.
- Contributors who land several quality changes can be offered commit rights. Decisions move to a majority of maintainers once there are three or more.
- Money received is used only for maintenance time, compute for training and CI, code signing, and hosting of model files. Income and spending are published monthly, see [docs/SPONSORS.md](docs/SPONSORS.md).
- Licenses and privacy promises (local by default, no accounts, no telemetry unless opted in) change only through a public issue with a comment period.
